# (126 mm down) against the span tendons' camber on the same span (114 mm

# simply supported 0.0130) and cambers ~1 mm: 5.9 mm net. The 15 mm
# between the two dead states is the construction sequence, not a
# discrepancy, and it is why comparing the two curves means comparing
