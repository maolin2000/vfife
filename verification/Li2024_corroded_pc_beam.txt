# Li, S. et al. (2024), "Experimental Study on Flexural Performance of
# Corrosion-Affected Simply Supported Prestressed Concrete T-Beams",
# Buildings 14, 3322.  https://doi.org/10.3390/buildings14103322  (CC BY)
#
# Eight 1:16.155 scaled T-beams, A0 to A7, post-tensioned with ONE
# 1x7-15.2-1860 strand in a grouted phi 25 bellows, the strand corroded by
# impressed current in 5% NaCl, then broken in four-point bending. Digitised
# from Tables 1 and 2, section 3.2 and Figures 1-3 of the PDF with
# `pdftotext -layout` and, for the figures, `pdftoppm -r 600` measured against
# the dimensioned section depth. Used by cases/case_31_li_control_beam.yaml.
#
# WHY THIS CAMPAIGN AND NOT KUANG'S CARRIES THE CAPACITY CHECK. Its table
# closes: 145.0 kN of applied load is exactly twice the 72.5 kN of MEASURED
# support reaction, and 72.5 x 0.850 = 61.63 kN m needs an internal lever arm
# of 0.200 m on a 240 mm beam -- attainable, and 83% of what the steel and the
# depth allow. Kuang's uncorroded beam needs 249.4 mm on a 250 mm beam, which
# is not; see Kuang2023_corroded_pc_beam.txt.
#
# The two campaigns are INDEPENDENT -- different authors, scale, spans and
# corrosion levels -- and they are not two measurements of the same thing. At
# a matched ~8% mass loss Kuang's beams lose 15.4% of capacity and these lose
# 27.7%, and the physical difference is the failure mode: Kuang's crush the
# concrete up to 6.28% and rupture the strand above, these are strand-
# controlled throughout.
#
# GEOMETRY (Figures 1 and 2, mm)
#   total length            2020        depth                  240
#   support spacing         1952        flange                 265 x 75
#   overhang each end         34        web, midspan            89
#   shear span               850        web, support           155
#   load-point spacing       252        elevation chain    34|59|186|1462|...
#   tendon above soffit   33 (mid)      so the web is full width for 93 mm and
#                         74 (support)  tapers over 186; the step idealisation
#                                       is placed at 279 from each end.
#   The paper publishes no section table. Integrating the figure:
#     midspan 3.456e4 mm2 / 1.642e8 mm4, centroid 151.5 mm above the soffit
#     support 4.545e4 mm2 / 2.284e8 mm4, centroid 135.0 mm
#
#   The tendon heights are the Figure 3 labels (33 and 74). Independent
#   checks: measuring the duct circle at 600 dpi against the dimensioned
#   240 mm depth gives 33.3 and 71.8; and at midspan the construction gives
#   it directly -- 20 mm bottom cover plus half of the 25 mm duct is 32.5.
#
# REINFORCEMENT (Figure 3)
#   prestress   1 x 1x7-15.2-1860, A = 140 mm2, phi 25 grouted metal bellows
#   flange      8 phi 8 deformed, in TWO layers of four inside a 225 mm stirrup
#   soffit      2 phi 8 deformed, flanking the duct at 20 mm cover
#   stirrups    phi 6 @ 100
#
# MATERIALS -- and what the paper does NOT give
#   concrete    "C50 high-performance", mix ratio 343:686:1072:147:88.2:58.2:4.9
#               -- IDENTICAL to Kuang's Table 2. No measured strength is
#               published. case_31 uses Kuang's measured f_cu = 45.24 MPa and
#               says so.
#   strand      "standard tensile strength 1860 MPa"; no measured values. The
#               product is the same 1x7-15.2-1860, so case_31 uses Kuang's
#               measured f_py 1834 / f_pu 1872 / E 1.95e5.
#   bars        phi 8 deformed, grade not stated for the longitudinal bars
#               (HRB-335 is named for the stirrups). case_31 uses HRB335.
#
# PRESTRESS
#   "The strand elongation of 12 mm was the final tension control index", over
#   a strand 2020 mm long -- so P = 12/2020 x 1.95e5 x 140 = 162 kN of jacking
#   force. (Kuang states 157 kN for a 2080 mm beam, the same procedure.) The
#   EFFECTIVE prestress is not published and there is no loss breakdown.
#
